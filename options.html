Your focus window

On a study webpage, click the extension icon once to start focus. It locks that exact page and keeps your video playing after you press play. Focus ends when the selected timer or the playing video finishes, whichever comes first.

Focus ends when the selected timer OR your playing video ends, whichever comes first. No early End button. Chrome still lets you disable the extension from chrome://extensions.

Right-click the extension icon > Options to reopen settings.
